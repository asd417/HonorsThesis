\section{NEAT Hyperparameter Sweep}
\label{app:neat_sweeps}

The following tables and figures show results from the two NEAT ablation sweeps.
Table~\ref{tab:neat_sweep_old} summarises the first sweep, which varied connection
deletion probability, node mutation rates, stagnation limit, and weight mutation
power across nine configurations. Table~\ref{tab:neat_sweep_new} shows the
second sweep, which refined the three pruning configurations with a corrected
full-dataset evaluation. All configurations used a population size of 150.

\centering

\textbf{Config} & \texttt{conn\_del} & \texttt{node\_add/del} & \texttt{stagnation} & \texttt{wt\_power} & \textbf{Solve Rate} \\
\midrule

prune\_light  & 0.3 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 15 & 0.5 & 84.7\% \\
