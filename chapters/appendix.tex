\section{NEAT Hyperparameter Sweep}
\label{app:neat_sweeps}

Table~\ref{tab:neat_sweep} summarizes the NEAT ablation sweep, which varied connection
deletion probability, node mutation rates, stagnation limit, and weight mutation
power across nine configurations. All configurations used a population size of 150.

\centering

\textbf{Config} & \texttt{conn\_del} & \texttt{node\_add/del} & \texttt{stagnation} & \texttt{wt\_power} & \textbf{Solve Rate} \\
\midrule
baseline    & 0.5 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 15 & 0.5 & 84.0\% \\
prune\_light  & 0.3 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 15 & 0.5 & 84.7\% \\
prune\_heavy  & 0.7 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 15 & 0.5 & 84.0\% \\
node\_high    & 0.5 & 0.4 / 0.4 & 15 & 0.5 & 84.0\% \\
node\_low     & 0.5 & 0.1 / 0.1 & 15 & 0.5 & 83.0\% \\
stag\_25      & 0.5 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 25 & 0.5 & 84.0\% \\
stag\_40      & 0.5 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 40 & 0.5 & 82.7\% \\
wt\_fine      & 0.5 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 15 & 0.3 & 84.4\% \\
wt\_coarse    & 0.5 & 0.2 / 0.2 & 15 & 0.8 & 84.7\% \\
